Lantern Math

The 365 lanterns at 8 lanterns a kit take 46 kits, the last kit holding 5 lanterns.

Boundary: arithmetic only Lantern Math counts an event's paper lanterns with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the kits the lanterns take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a lantern, and the minutes the hanging takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your lanterns, your kits or your venue and does not know whether the lanterns will really hang straight, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a kit is worth its price or a lantern is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 365 lanterns, 8 lanterns a kit, 30 lanterns a week, $12.75 a kit and 5 lanterns a minute.
