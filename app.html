Lantern Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's paper lanterns with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the kits the lanterns take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a lantern, and the minutes the hanging takes. It uses no named constants. The lanterns, the kit size, the weekly use, the kit price and the hanging speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your lanterns, your kits or your venue and does not know whether the lanterns will really hang straight, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a kit is worth its price or a lantern is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The kits truth

Kits: the lanterns divided by the lanterns a kit, rounded up, with what the last kit holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the lanterns divided by the lanterns used a week, with the lanterns left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the kits times the kit price, to the cent, and spread over the lanterns.

The time truth

Time: the lanterns divided by the lanterns hung a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Lantern Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
